# Human Gates

## GATE 1 — Truth
Owner/client must approve or supply documentary evidence for:
- legal name;
- EIN;
- federal tax status / fiscal sponsor;
- charitable-registration status;
- service area;
- board/governance;
- current program;
- outcomes;
- partner names;
- participant/media permissions;
- safeguarding claims.

## GATE 2 — Public claims
No unverified legal, tax, funding, partner, outcome, participant, safety, or program claim is published.

## GATE 3 — Production
Production domain/DNS/canonical deployment cutover requires owner approval plus rollback packet.

## GATE 4 — Destructive consolidation
Archiving/deleting repos, domains, deployments, buckets, databases, or client data requires explicit approval.

## GATE 5 — External consequences
Grant submission, money movement, legal filings, donor/sponsor outreach, youth/family communication, or public publication with material consequences requires approval.

## GATE 6 — Handoff
Final acceptance requires:
- owner map;
- training;
- recovery/rollback;
- verified deliverables;
- unresolved-items list;
- written acceptance.
